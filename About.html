ABOUT TECHFEST

# About NBKRIST Tech Fest **NBKRIST Tech Fest** is one of the most exciting and prestigious annual technical festivals organized by **NBKR Institute of Science and Technology (NBKRIST)**. The festival is designed to inspire innovation, creativity, and technical excellence among students from various engineering and technology disciplines. It serves as a platform where young minds come together to exchange ideas, demonstrate their talents, and gain practical exposure to modern The Tech Fest brings together students, faculty members, researchers, industry professionals, and technology enthusiasts from different colleges. It provides an excellent opportunity for participants to enhance their technical knowledge, improve problem-solving abilities, and develop teamwork and leadership skills through healthy competition. The festival features a wide range of technical and non-technical events that encourage students to think creatively and apply their classroom knowledge to real-world challenges. Participants can showcase their innovative ideas through project exhibitions, coding competitions, hackathons, robotics contests, technical quizzes, paper presentations, poster presentations, circuit designing, web development challenges, debugging contests, and many other exciting activities. These events help students improve their confidence while learning the latest trends in technology. One of the major highlights of the Tech Fest is the series of workshops and expert sessions conducted by industry professionals and academicians. These sessions introduce participants to emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Cyber Security, Cloud Computing, Internet of Things (IoT), Blockchain, Full Stack Development, and Software Engineering. Students gain valuable insights into current industry practices and future career opportunities. Apart from technical competitions, the festival also includes fun events, cultural performances, gaming competitions, photography contests, treasure hunts, and entertainment programs. These activities create a vibrant atmosphere where students can relax, interact, and build friendships with participants from different institutions. NBKRIST Tech Fest aims to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements by promoting innovation, research, and entrepreneurship. It motivates students to think beyond textbooks, develop practical solutions, and contribute to technological advancements that benefit society. The festival also provides opportunities for students to network with professionals, recruiters, startups, and alumni. Such interactions help participants understand industry expectations, improve communication skills, and explore internship and placement opportunities. With attractive prizes, certificates, recognition, and unforgettable learning experiences, NBKRIST Tech Fest has become a celebration of technology, creativity, collaboration, and excellence. Every year, hundreds of enthusiastic students participate in the festival with passion and determination, making it one of the most awaited events on the college calendar. **Vision of the Tech Fest** * To encourage innovation and creativity among students. * To provide practical exposure to emerging technologies. * To promote research, technical excellence, and entrepreneurship. * To develop teamwork, leadership, and problem-solving skills. * To create opportunities for interaction with industry experts. * To inspire students to build solutions for real-world challenges. **Theme** *"Innovating Today for a Smarter Tomorrow."* **Join the Celebration!** Whether you are a programmer, designer, innovator, researcher, or technology enthusiast, NBKRIST Tech Fest welcomes you to learn, compete, collaborate, and create memorable experiences. Come, showcase your talent, expand your knowledge, and be a part of the future of technology.
